SEO Fundamentals

Off Page SEO Basics Guide

Master the fundamentals of off-page SEO including backlinks, citations, and building authority for your website in Nepal.

Published: April 25, 2026 | Reading time: 9 minutes

What is Off-Page SEO?

Off-page SEO refers to all SEO activities that happen outside your website. While on-page SEO involves optimizing your content, titles, and structure, off-page SEO is about building your website's authority and reputation across the internet. Off-page SEO signals tell Google that your website is trustworthy and valuable.

Understanding Backlinks

Backlinks are links from other websites to yours. They are the most important off-page SEO factor. When another website links to you, it's like a vote of confidence saying "this site is worth visiting."

Quality over quantity: One backlink from a high-authority website (like a major news site) is worth more than 100 links from low-quality sites.

Natural backlinks: The best backlinks are earned naturally when people genuinely want to link to your content. Avoid buying backlinks or participating in link schemes, as Google penalizes these practices.

How to Get Quality Backlinks

  • Create valuable content: Write in-depth blog posts that people want to link to. Think about what questions your customers ask and answer them thoroughly.
  • Reach out to relevant websites: Find websites, blogs, and directories in your industry and ask if they'd link to your content.
  • Guest posting: Write articles for established websites in your niche and include a link back to your site in your author bio.
  • Business directories: Submit your website to reputable business directories (Google My Business, local Nepal directories).
  • Local partnerships: Partner with other Kathmandu businesses and ask for links on their websites.
  • Press releases: When you have news about your business, write a press release and distribute it to news websites.

Citations and Local SEO

Citations are mentions of your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) on other websites. They're especially important for local SEO in Nepal:

  • Google My Business: This is the most important citation. Make sure your information is complete and consistent.
  • Local directories: List your business on Nepali business directories, classified sites, and review platforms.
  • Consistency matters: Use the exact same business name, address, and phone number everywhere. Inconsistencies confuse Google.
  • Relevant citations: Citations on relevant websites (fashion, retail, local business sites) are more valuable than random websites.

Building Social Proof

While social media shares aren't direct ranking factors, they help build authority and drive traffic:

  • Social signals: Shares, likes, and comments on social media indicate that your content is valuable and engaging.
  • Brand visibility: The more your brand appears on social media, the more people recognize and trust you.
  • Traffic generation: Social media drives traffic to your website, which improves rankings.
  • Customer reviews: Positive reviews on Google and other platforms build authority and influence rankings.

Brand Mentions and Reviews

Brand mentions—when someone mentions your business online—are important for SEO:

  • Google reviews: Encourage customers to leave positive reviews on your Google Business Profile.
  • Local review sites: Get listed and reviewed on Nepali business review platforms.
  • Industry mentions: When fashion blogs or publications mention your brand, it builds authority.
  • Monitor mentions: Use Google Alerts to track when your business is mentioned online.

Competitor Backlink Analysis

Learn from your competitors' backlinks:

  • Identify who links to your competitors
  • Reach out to those same websites with your content
  • Find broken links on relevant websites and suggest your content as a replacement
  • Look for high-authority sites in your industry that you haven't been linked from yet

Off-Page SEO Best Practices

Quality first: Focus on getting links and mentions from reputable, relevant sources.

Natural growth: Build links gradually. Sudden spikes in backlinks look suspicious to Google.

Diverse sources: Get backlinks from different types of websites (blogs, directories, news sites).

Anchor text matters: The text used to link to you (anchor text) should be relevant. "Click here" links are less valuable than keyword-rich anchor text.

Be consistent: Maintain the same business information (name, address, phone) everywhere online.

What NOT to Do

Don't buy links: Google penalizes websites that buy backlinks or participate in link schemes.

Don't use private blog networks (PBNs): Networks of fake websites created just to provide backlinks are against Google's rules.

Don't exchange links excessively: Trading links with many websites just for the sake of linking looks unnatural.

Don't use low-quality directories: Links from spam directories can actually hurt your SEO.

Timeline for Results

Off-page SEO takes time. You typically won't see significant ranking improvements for 2-3 months. However, consistent effort in building quality backlinks, citations, and brand mentions will compound over time and lead to better rankings in 6-12 months.

Conclusion

Off-page SEO is about building your website's reputation and authority across the internet. Focus on creating valuable content that people want to link to, maintain consistent business information everywhere, and build genuine relationships with other websites in your industry. Combined with good on-page SEO, a strong off-page SEO strategy will help your website rank higher in Google and attract more customers in Nepal.
